Read section 3 as the real generalisation test. A positive LORO R2
means loading measured in OTHER river systems predicts chemistry in
one it has never seen - that is what 'our map beats Rockwell's' would
actually have to mean to support a grant claim. Section 1's pooled rho
alone is NOT sufficient evidence by itself; a result that only shows
up in section 1 and collapses in sections 2-3 is a pooling artefact,
not a finding, and must be reported as such.
